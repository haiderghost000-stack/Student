---
name: PDF Arabic zoom rendering
description: Keeps the Arabic PDF text layer stable while pages zoom and rerender.
---

When displaying Arabic through PDF.js TextLayer, set the Arabic fallback font in the text-content style map before constructing the layer so PDF.js measures and renders with the same font. Do not override the span font in CSS after layout. Serialize or cancel prior canvas and text-layer renders before reusing a page's canvas and DOM. Render zoom updates into temporary canvas/text-layer buffers and swap them in only after both finish, so the last good page stays visible. Capture the zoom focal point as page-relative coordinates, then restore it after layout; raw scroll-ratio math loses the focal point when pages are centered or padded. For pinch preview, use Panzoom on the page stack; disable native touch panning for the gesture and implement one-finger scrolling manually, or browser scrolling can race pinch transforms and shift the vertical anchor. On pinch end, capture the current focal point, clear Panzoom's transform, rerender PDF.js at the new scale, and restore the anchor after layout. Disable browser scroll anchoring and provide temporary scroll room around short pages so the scroll container can apply the correction.

**Why:** A CSS-only font override changes glyph widths after PDF.js has calculated span scaling; zoom redraws can fragment or misalign text. Re-rendering on every touchmove interrupts the pinch gesture and overlaps cancelled page renders, while painting directly into the visible canvas makes zoom look like a page refresh. Centered stack margins and padding invalidate scroll formulas that assume content starts at the scroll origin, and short documents may have too little scroll range to keep a page-relative anchor in place. Panzoom's transform alone cannot prevent the browser from also scrolling after the first finger touches down.

**How to apply:** Follow this rule when changing `PdfReader` Arabic shaping, font selection, pinch handling, or zoom rendering; keep the current rendered page visible until the replacement render is complete, disable native touch/scroll anchoring during gesture correction, preserve one-finger scrolling, and make enough temporary scroll space to retain the exact page spot.
